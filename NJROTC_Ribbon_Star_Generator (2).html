Credit requirements per award
  • Academic Team: 3 credits
  • C.E.R.T.: 1 credit
  • Color Guard: 3 credits
  • Community Service: 10 credits
  • Drill Team: 3 credits
  • Inter-Service Competition: 3 credits
  • Leadership Training: 1 credit
  • Participation: 3 credits
  • S.T.E.M.: 3 credits
  • Unit Service: 10 credits

These requirements are fixed in the generator and cannot be edited on this page.

Other conversion rules
  • Undefined rules: qualifying activities, how credits are assigned in the source report, and category-specific eligibility limits are not defined here. Verify them against unit policy before issuing awards.
  • Partial awards are rounded down; credit categories are not combined.
  • 1 award = ribbon; awards 2–5 add bronze stars; awards 6–9 add silver stars; awards 10–13 add gold stars. More than 13 total awards are flagged for manual review.
Privacy: PDF processing runs in this browser; the file is not uploaded to a server by this page. Internet access is needed to load the PDF and Excel libraries.
